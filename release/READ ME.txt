Orbital Launcher 2.0
====================

TO INSTALL
1. Right-click the ZIP you downloaded and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Install Orbital Launcher.bat".
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Thats it. Nothing else to download. The app goes into your user folder
(no admin rights needed), with an icon on the desktop and in the Start menu.
Press Alt+Space anywhere to open the ring.

TO UPDATE
Download the new version and run its installer. Your settings are kept.

TO REMOVE
Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Orbital Launcher > Uninstall.
